## 🚀 Featured Work

### ⚡ KPX Short-Term Electricity Demand Forecasting | MLOps

`Python` `LightGBM` `BigQuery` `Cloud Run` `Cloud Scheduler` `GCP` `SQL`

Led the MODELBRIDGE capstone team building a pipeline that generates **24 demand forecasts every 15 minutes**, covering the next 15 to 360 minutes. The team addressed data-timing issues, checked for look-ahead leakage, and compared the new model with the existing one under matched live conditions.

- **48-hour live validation:** WMAPE **4.1837% → 3.7032%** (approximately **11.5% relative improvement**; 4,608 predictions per model).
- LightGBM v3 was promoted to the team's production model on **October 7, 2026**.

<sub>Capstone team project · Results reflect a documented 48-hour evaluation window.</sub>

### 🛒 eBay Operations Automation | Palantir Foundry

`Palantir Foundry` `Ontology` `AIP` `Actions` `Human-in-the-loop`

Designed an e-commerce workflow around connected product, order, and shipping data. Used **Ontology** to organize business context and **AIP** to assist with repetitive shipping and product inquiries. Pricing and margin calculations supported proposed price changes, with final approval kept in human hands through **Actions**.

- Routine inquiries requiring a manual response went from approximately **30 to 11 per week** (about **63% fewer**, based on my own operating records).

<sub>Independent e-commerce project · Operational data and implementation are not publicly shared.</sub>

### 🧠 Local AI Academic Advisor | RAG Application

`Python` `Streamlit` `Ollama` `LangChain` `FAISS` `MySQL`

Built a local AI assistant prototype combining rule-based graduation requirement checks with retrieval-augmented academic Q&A. The system brings together document retrieval, an on-device LLM, and a web interface.

<sub>Prototype project · Not a university-wide deployed service.</sub>

### 📈 XGBoost + PPO | Futures Trading Research

`Python` `XGBoost` `Reinforcement Learning` `Backtesting`

Co-first-authored research combining XGBoost's estimated entry-success probability with PPO reinforcement learning to control trading entries. The study reported a **70.26% simulated return** and **−24.04% maximum drawdown** under its backtesting setup.

[Read the paper (PDF)](https://d2j16w31g89z0j.cloudfront.net/site/2026w/abs/0856-XXCVU.pdf) · <sub>Simulation results, not live investment performance.</sub>
